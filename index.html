Patterns

The operational failures that keep recurring.

These aren't unique problems. They're patterns — and patterns can be fixed with the right structure.

Explore the notes →
pattern · CRM

The CRM nobody trusts.

Stages that don't match real workflow states. Fields that mean different things to different people. Data that reflects what was entered, not what actually happened.

  • Pipeline stages aren't reflecting actual work
  • Fields are inconsistent across records
  • Reports show history, not current reality
  • Nobody trusts the data, so nobody updates it
pattern · escalation

The escalation tax nobody measures.

Senior staff interrupted constantly. Same questions answered again and again. Every escalation costs not just five minutes — it costs the switching time too.

  • 10 interruptions/day = 3+ hours of senior time lost
  • Knowledge stays in one person's head
  • No documented path for edge cases
  • Dependency builds instead of shrinks over time
pattern · handoffs

Handoffs are where work goes to die.

The work is done on one end. The context doesn't transfer. The next person starts over, makes wrong assumptions, or stalls the whole thing waiting for a clarification.

  • Artifact passes without the reasoning behind it
  • Receiving person lacks status context
  • Edge cases handled but never communicated
  • Follow-up questions eat back all the time saved
